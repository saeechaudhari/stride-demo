Why these categories appear

How STRIDE should rank products

  • Race distance, expected duration and training-session timing
  • Food plan first: products fill convenience or fuelling needs, not assumed deficiencies
  • Diet preference, allergies and cross-contact risk
  • Health conditions, medication and clinician restrictions
  • Ingredient transparency, serving size, evidence and third-party testing
  • Budget, taste tolerance, verified availability and user feedback
Low ferritin or vitamin D results should prompt appropriate clinical follow-up—not automatic high-dose supplement recommendations.
