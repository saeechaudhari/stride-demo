How STRIDE should rank providers

  • Google rating and review count together, not rating alone.
  • Review recency, themes, detailed athlete feedback and owner responses.
  • Credentials, relevant sport/event experience and facility/equipment evidence.
  • Transparent price, location, availability, cancellation and booking path.

Booking & safety checks

What overlaps with Product Guidance?

Fuel gels, hydration mixes, electrolyte products and recovery nutrition remain in Product Guidance. This marketplace focuses on physical gear and accessories, but may link back to nutrition products when they are part of the race checklist.

STRIDE should deduplicate the catalogue by product ID so one item can appear in multiple useful contexts without being counted as two separate products.

Before you buy

  • Prioritise gear already tested during training.
  • Check sizing, fit, return policy and compatibility.
  • For high-altitude or remote races, review required kit and organiser guidance.
  • Don't buy a device solely to get a fitness score; use existing data if it meets the need.
  • Price, stock and product links need live verification before production.
